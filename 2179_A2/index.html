Life in the Digital Age

Understanding how modern screen habits are changing the way we use our eyes and influencing long-term eye health.

Through data visualization, we observe that modern screen habits clearly influence eye health scores. Higher screen time is strongly associated with lower eye health outcomes. This suggests that digital lifestyle patterns are reshaping how we use our eyes in daily life. Poor habits such as excessive screen exposure may increase long term eye strain and potential vision risks.

Although modern habits increasingly focus on screen-based entertainment, many studies still encourage more time outdoors and physical exercise. The data supports this, showing that higher outdoor exposure is associated with better eye health scores.
