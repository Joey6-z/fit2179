Life in the Digital Age

Understanding how modern screen habits are influencing long-term eye health.

Through data visualization, we observe that modern screen habits clearly influence eye health scores. Higher screen time is strongly associated with lower eye health outcomes. This suggests that digital lifestyle patterns are reshaping how we use our eyes in daily life. Poor habits such as excessive screen exposure may increase long term eye strain and potential vision risks.

Although modern habits increasingly focus on screen-based entertainment, many studies still encourage more time outdoors and physical exercise. The data supports this, showing that higher outdoor exposure is associated with better eye health scores.

A World Losing Sight

Tracking the global spread of vision impairment and the rising burden of sight loss over time.

This map shows the global distribution of vision impairment by severity level, revealing clear regional inequalities. Higher burdens are often found in lower-income regions, especially in South Asia and sub-Saharan Africa.

This dataset reflects trends in vision impairment in Australia, showing a clear upward pattern across all severity levels over time. Both male and female populations follow similar trajectories, suggesting that shared environmental and lifestyle factors are influencing eye health nationwide.

Among all categories, moderate-severe cases remain the most common, indicating that the major burden of vision loss lies in long-term functional impairment rather than extreme blindness.

The Future of Global Vision Health

Predicting how sight loss cases may continue to rise over time.

This projection highlights the future trajectory of global vision health, showing a consistent upward trend across all population groups.
The total, female, and male groups all demonstrate similar growth patterns, suggesting that the increasing burden of vision loss is a global phenomenon driven by shared environmental and lifestyle changes.

What the slope chart makes clear is this: unless action is taken, the number of people at risk of vision impairment will only continue to rise in the years ahead.

Hidden Cause of Vision Loss

Understanding the factors behind vision loss trends.

This chart reveals the hidden cost of vision loss. One thing stands out clearly: cataract makes up the largest share. Looking back to earlier patterns, not all vision loss comes from disease alone. A large part is linked to everyday lifestyle and eye health habits. This is where moderate vision loss often appears.

It reflects people who are still living with manageable vision issues, but slowly affected by long-term health and screen-related strain.

Other causes like blindness take a smaller share, but remain important. Overall, the pattern shows a mix of medical conditions and lifestyle-driven risks.
